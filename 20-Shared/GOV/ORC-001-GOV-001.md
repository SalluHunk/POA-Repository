# Delegated Authority

The Chief Navigator & Architecture Steward is delegated operational authority to continue implementation within the approved Genesis scope without requiring repeated executive authorization.

From this point onward, the Chief Navigator & Architecture Steward is authorized to independently:

## ✅ Execute Approved Workstreams

- Continue Visual DNA
- Continue Design System
- Continue Wireframes
- Continue Experience Blueprint
- Continue Genesis Experience
- Continue Engineering Foundation

## ✅ Manage the Program

- Prioritize tasks
- Sequence work
- Identify dependencies
- Prepare implementation packages
- Conduct internal architectural reviews

## ✅ Protect the Architecture

The Chief Navigator & Architecture Steward is expected to stop, redesign, or postpone implementation if it violates:

- Constitution
- Governance
- Design Philosophy
- Genesis Scope
- Architectural Coherence

That stewardship is part of the Chief Navigator & Architecture Steward's mandate.

---

# Matters Reserved for Commander

The following always remain under the Commander's authority:

- Constitutional changes
- New architectural layers
- Commercial strategy
- Product vision
- Major scope expansion
- Budget and organizational decisions
- Release approval

This keeps executive leadership where it belongs.

# GOV-003 Addendum — Authority Formalization (2026-08-11)

**This section is NOT part of the verbatim source `.docx` reproduction above.** It is an additive amendment, appended per `CLAUDE.md`'s historical-integrity discipline (`POA-INTEGRITY-CONTROL-001` §L(b)) and the precedent set by `40-Runtime/REPO-AUTHORITY-RECONCILIATION-REPORT.md` §16 ("Addendum — Commander Disposition"). It formally records three Commander decisions communicated via `CHIEF ARCHITECT DIRECTIVE — GOV-003.md`, following the authority-ambiguity findings of `GOV-001` and `GOV-002`. No word of the verbatim content above is altered; the Artifact Identity table above (including Status and Materialized By) is unchanged and remains accurate to the original source document.

## 1. "Chief Architect" Terminology

"Chief Architect" is the operational architectural title/function of the formally governed **Chief Navigator & Architecture Steward** defined above. It is **not** a separate governance role, and this addendum does not create a new constitutional office. Wherever repository practice has used "Chief Architect" (directive titles, in-session acceptance grants — see `SR-001`, `INT-001`, `RSR-001`, `POA-VIS-003`/`004` completion reports), that usage refers to the same role and authority already delegated above, under an operational name.

## 2. Certification Authority

Certification authority — as defined and exercised through `20-Shared/GOV/ACS-001.md` — is delegated to the Chief Navigator & Architecture Steward, as an extension of the "Manage the Program" and "Protect the Architecture" delegations already granted above. This delegation does not expand the Steward's authority into any matter reserved to Commander (see "Matters Reserved for Commander," above); it names certification specifically as falling within the Steward's existing delegated scope, resolving the ambiguity `GOV-001`/`GOV-002` identified.

## 3. Commander Supremacy Over Certification

Notwithstanding §2, the Commander retains supreme authority over certification and may, at Commander's discretion:

- **Reserve** a certification decision to Commander rather than the Steward.
- **Escalate** a pending certification review to Commander for direct decision.
- **Override** a certification decision the Steward has made.
- **Withhold** certification despite a Steward recommendation to grant.
- **Revoke** a certification the Steward has previously granted.

This is a statement of principle, consistent with the "Matters Reserved for Commander" authority already established above. The **procedural mechanics** of reservation/escalation (triggers, notice, timing) are not specified by the Commander decision this addendum records and remain a governance dependency — not invented here.
